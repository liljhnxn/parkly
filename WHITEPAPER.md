## 1. Executive Summary

Urban parking is notoriously fragmented, inefficient, and expensive. Drivers spend millions of hours circling congested city centers searching for parking, while private residential and commercial spaces remain underutilized. Existing Web2 parking aggregators charge predatory convenience fees (15%–35%), harvest sensitive consumer location data, and operate opaque, centralized databases prone to phantom bookings and double reservation errors.

**Parkly** is an open, trustless, and decentralized parking infrastructure protocol deployed on the **Botchain** blockchain. Parkly enables private and commercial parking space owners to seamlessly tokenize the availability of physical parking slots and monetize them directly in BOHR. Drivers can explore real-time availability and lock in cryptographically verified reservations without intermediaries, account lock-in, or hidden commissions.

## 2. Problem Statement

### 2.1 The Urban Mobility Challenge
* **Congestion & Emissions:** Research indicates that up to 30% of inner-city traffic is generated by motorists actively searching for vacant parking.
* **Capital Inefficiency:** Millions of private residential driveways, office lots outside business hours, and small business parking bays remain vacant due to lack of a friction-free marketplace.

### 2.2 Centralized Marketplace Failure Modes
* **High Intermediary Rent-Seeking:** Traditional platforms take massive percentage cuts from both property owners and drivers.
* **Privacy Intrusions & Surveillance:** Web2 parking apps require invasive personal identity checks, phone numbers, license plate profiling, and financial tracking.
* **Double Booking & Disputed Bookings:** Centralized off-chain databases often experience sync lag, leading to multiple drivers arriving at the exact same physical space.

### 4.2 Data Models

```solidity
struct ParkingSpace {
    uint256 id;
    address owner;
    string spaceNumber;
    string location;
    uint256 hourlyRate;
    SpaceStatus status;     // Available (0), Occupied (1), Unavailable (2)
    uint256 createdAt;
}

struct Reservation {
    uint256 id;
    uint256 spaceId;
    address driver;
    uint256 startTime;
    uint256 endTime;
    ReservationStatus status; // Active (0), Cancelled (1), Completed (2)
}
```

### 4.3 Anti-Overlap Mathematical Specification
A reservation request with time interval $[T_{start}, T_{end})$ is evaluated against all existing active reservations $[R_{start}, R_{end})$ for that specific parking slot. An overlap occurs if and only if:

$$\max(T_{start}, R_{start}) < \min(T_{end}, R_{end})$$

Which simplifies to:
$$T_{start} < R_{end} \quad \land \quad T_{end} > R_{start}$$

If this condition evaluates to true for any active reservation on that space ID, the smart contract reverts with the custom error `ReservationOverlap(r.id, r.startTime, r.endTime)`.

### 4.4 Status & In-Progress Protection
* A space owner can toggle availability between `Available` and `Unavailable`.
* If an active reservation is currently underway ($R_{start} \le \text{block.timestamp} < R_{end}$), the contract forbids marking the slot as `Available`, preventing operational race conditions.

## 5. Security & Privacy Philosophy

1. **Zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** Parkly does not collect or store user names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical vehicle license plates.
2. **Access Control Modifiers:** Ownership checks (`onlySpaceOwner`) and authorization boundaries prevent unauthorized cancellations or malicious parameter edits.
3. **Anti-Self-Dealing:** Space owners are cryptographically blocked from reserving their own parking slots (`CannotReserveOwnSpace`).
4. **Verified Codebase:** The entire smart contract bytecode is verified on [BohrScan](https://scan.bohr.life/address/0xE00857F9e625ec7D7272febc750C7391cfeC7f6F#code).

## 6. Ecosystem & Economic Model

* **Native Currency:** Transactions and hourly valuations are denominated in `BOHR`, driving fundamental utility and transaction volume to the Botchain network.
* **Platform Fee:** 0% protocol rent during testnet and initial adoption, establishing network effects.
* **Scalability:** Gas-efficient custom Solidity errors keep state manipulation costs minimal.
