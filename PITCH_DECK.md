## Slide 1: Cover & Mission
* **Product:** PARKLY
* **Mission:** Decentralizing urban parking infrastructure on Botchain.
* **Core Value:** Enabling property owners to tokenize and monetize vacant parking bays while empowering drivers to reserve verified spots with instant cryptographic finality.

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
1. **Urban Congestion:** 30% of traffic in downtown metropolitan areas is generated by drivers looking for empty parking spots.
2. **Underutilized Real Estate:** Private residential driveways, corporate lots during off-hours, and retail parking bays sit completely idle.
3. **Predatory Web2 Middlemen:** Centralized apps take 20% to 35% commission fees, impose complex payout delays, and suffer from double-booking sync failures.
4. **Surveillance & Data Exploitation:** Drivers are forced to submit sensitive payment credentials, phone numbers, and physical license plates just to park for an hour.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (Parkly)
* **Peer-to-Peer Parking Economy:** Anyone can list an open parking bay in under 60 seconds.
* **Mathematical Overlap Prevention:** Built-in EVM logic prevents double bookings at the protocol level.
* **Frictionless Web3 Payments:** Direct BOHR native transactions with zero middleman markups.
* **Privacy by Default:** Zero personal identifiable information (PII) collected or stored.

---

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity
* **Global Parking Management Market:** Valued at \$10.5 Billion in 2024, projected to reach \$23.8 Billion by 2032 (CAGR 10.8%).
* **Real-World Asset (RWA) Tokenization:** Parking capacity is one of the highest-frequency, everyday physical assets ready for on-chain coordination.
* **Target Audience:** Urban commuters, event attendees, homeowners near entertainment districts, and commercial facility managers.

---

## Slide 5: Product Walkthrough & Architecture
1. **Public Discovery:** Drivers browse live parking cards and filter by availability without needing to connect a wallet.
2. **Instant On-Chain Listing:** Owners connect MetaMask and enter space number, location, and hourly rate.
3. **Time-Window Reservations:** Drivers select local start/end times with dynamic fee calculation and automated conflict warnings.
4. **Lifecycle Control:** Active bookings can be cancelled by authorized parties or marked completed by the property owner upon vehicle departure.

---

## Slide 7: Business Model & Monetization
* **Phase 1 (Growth):** 0% platform fee to bootstrap supply and liquidity of parking spaces across key metropolitan hubs.
* **Phase 2 (Sustainable Protocol Fee):** Optional 1% to 2% protocol treasury fee on confirmed bookings.
* **Phase 3 (Enterprise & Hardware):** Smart IoT parking barrier arm kits and automated RFID/BLE verification hardware for commercial garages.
